About Me

I'm a postdoc researcher at the Chair of Logistics in the Department of Business and Economics of the RPTU Kaiserslautern-Landau My research focuses on Operations Research, particularly Vehicle Routing Problems, Facility Location, Exact and Metaheuristic Algorithms, and Stochastic Programming. I am also a member of the board of EUROYoung.

This Blog

This website brings together resources related to my research, including publications, preprints, computational codes, presentations, and talks. You will also find occasional posts about my interests and activities beyond academia.
