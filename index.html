About Me

I am a Research Assistant at RPTU Kaiserslautern , at the Chair of Logistics in the Department of Business and Economics. My research focuses on Operations Research, particularly Vehicle Routing Problems, Facility Location, Metaheuristic Algorithms, and Stochastic Programming. I am also a member of the board of EUROYoung .

About This Website

This website brings together resources related to my research, including preprints, computational codes, presentations, and talks. You will also find occasional posts about my interests and activities beyond academia.
