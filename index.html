About Me

I am a Research Assistant at RPTU Kaiserslautern , at the Chair of Logistics in the Department of Business and Economics. My research focuses on Operations Research, particularly Vehicle Routing, Facility Location, Metaheuristic Algorithms, and Stochastic Programming.

I am also a member of the board of EUROYoung.

About This Website

This website brings together resources related to my research, including preprints, computational codes, presentations, and talks. I also share occasional content about my interests outside academia.
