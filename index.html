About Me

I'm a postdoc researcher at RPTU kaiserslautern (chair of Logistics of the Department of Bussines and Economics), my special domain is Operations Research. at the Chair of Logistics in the Department of Business and Economics. My research focuses on Operations Research, particularly Vehicle Routing Problems, Facility Location, Metaheuristic Algorithms, and Stochastic Programming. I am also a member of the board of EUROYoung.

This Blog

This website brings together resources related to my research, including publications, preprints, computational codes, presentations, and talks. You will also find occasional posts about my interests and activities beyond academia.
