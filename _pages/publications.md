## Research at a Glance

My research focuses on location-routing problems, stochastic optimization,
combinatorial optimization, and metaheuristics. Current work emphasizes
exact and heuristic solution methods for large-scale logistics and
transportation systems.

- Publications in *European Journal of Operational Research*, *Computers & Operations Research*, *International Transactions in Operational Research*, *Transportation Research Part E*, *Networks*, and *OR Spectrum*.
- Research collaborations across Europe and Latin America.
- Contributions spanning location-routing, latency optimization, stochastic programming, and vehicle routing.
